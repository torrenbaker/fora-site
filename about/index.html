About fora

Legal entity: Fora Holding LLC  ·  Formed: 2026  ·  Based in: Arizona, USA
Official website: withfora.com  ·  Contact: support@withfora.com  ·  Platform: iOS, 18+

fora shows men how their dating profile actually lands. A man submits the six photos and three prompts he already runs. Five verified women each score every item on a scale from −5 to +5 and pick one preset reason for each score. He gets all ten verdicts back with the reasons attached. There is no matching, no messaging, no chat and no swiping anywhere in the product. It is a mirror, not a dating app.

2.fora is not a verification service

This one matters, so it is worth being blunt about.

  • fora will never contact you first. Not by text, not by email, not through a dating app. If a message from “fora” arrives out of the blue, it is not from us.
  • fora does not verify you to anyone else. We are not a safety badge, a background check, a dating pass, or an ID service. Nothing we do makes you “verified” to a person you matched with.
  • No legitimate match will ever need you to sign up here before meeting. There is no version of fora that would give someone a reason to send you a link.

There is a widespread scam in which someone you met on a dating app insists you get “verified” on an outside website before they will meet you. The link goes to a lookalike site that harvests your card details or personal information. Scammers borrow real brand names to make the request sound safe, and they often send an address that is close to a real one rather than the real one.

If anyone asks you to verify your identity on any website to prove you are real, it is a scam. That is true regardless of which brand name they invoke, including ours. The FTC has guidance on romance scams worth reading.

4.Why we built it

Men rewrite their bios and reshuffle their photos for months without ever learning what the women seeing that profile actually think. The feedback loop is broken: no matches is a signal with no explanation attached. Friends are too kind to be useful, and AI tools can only guess at an answer that belongs to real people.

fora closes that loop with the smallest honest mechanism we could design. Five women, ten items, one score and one reason each, no free-text box anywhere so nothing can turn into an insult. When five strangers who cannot see each other's answers all land in the same place, that stops being an opinion and starts being information.
