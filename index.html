Trust & safety

Honest by design. Safe by architecture.

Real-person verified

Every reviewer and every submitter passes a selfie check. No bots, no catfish, both directions.

No free text, ever

Feedback is a score plus a preset reason. The language to write an insult doesn't exist in the product.

Anonymity, enforced

Reviews display only when two or more reviewers agree — no single voice is ever identifiable.

FAQ

Fair questions.

Is it anonymous?+
Yes — absolutely, in both directions. He never learns who reviewed him; reviews only display once at least two reviewers agree, so no single opinion is ever identifiable. And his profile is only ever seen by his panel — never public, never browsable.
Will people be mean?+
They can't write anything. Reviews are a score and a choice from preset reasons — there's no free text anywhere. A score can be honestly negative; it can't be an insult.
Who reviews my profile?+
Five verified women, each rating independently — every photo, every prompt. What you get back is a real spread of takes, not one loud opinion.
How long does it take?+
Usually within a day — it depends on the queue. You'll see your panel fill in as reviews land.
What does it cost?+
fora runs on credits. Your welcome grant covers your first runs, so you'll see a full read before you ever pay. Reviewers never pay for anything — they earn.
