How it works

Build the lineup. Meet the panel.

Rebuild the profile you already run: six photos, three prompts, your vitals. It goes to five verified women who score every item and pick one preset reason each. They see it the way a match would — name, age, photos, prompts. What they don't get is each other's answers or a box to type in: every score is locked in blind, and the only words available are preset reasons.

Honest by design. Safe by architecture.

Real-person verified

Every reviewer and every submitter passes a selfie check. No bots, no catfish, both directions.

No free text, ever

Feedback is a score plus a preset reason. The language to write an insult doesn't exist in the product.

Reviewers are never shown

He gets all five scores and knows each came from a verified woman. He never learns which one gave which. No name, no photo, no profile.

Every photo scanned

All images are content-scanned before they are ever stored. Nothing unsafe enters the deck.

Fair questions.

Is it anonymous?+
The reviewers are, completely. He sees all five scores and the reasons behind them, and he knows every one came from a verified woman — but never which woman gave which score. Reviewers are never shown to him: no name, no photo, no profile. Reviewers do see his profile the way a match would, name and age included — and his profile goes only to his panel of five — never to a public feed and never browsable.
Will people be mean?+
They can't write anything. Reviews are a score and a choice from preset reasons, and there is no free text anywhere. A score can be honestly negative. It cannot be an insult.
Who reviews my profile?+
Five verified women, each rating independently, every photo and every prompt. What you get back is a real spread of takes rather than one loud opinion.
How is fora different from AI profile reviews?+
AI tools guess what women think. fora asks them. Every read comes from five real, verified women rating independently, the same people your profile is actually trying to reach.
How long does it take?+
Usually within a day, depending on the queue. You'll see your panel fill in as reviews land.
What does it cost?+
fora runs on credits. Your welcome grant covers your first runs, so you'll see a full read before you ever pay.
