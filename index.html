For reviewers

Your opinion, finally worth something.

Judging dating profiles is genuinely fun — fora just makes it count. Open the deck, and the next profile is waiting: slide a score under each photo and prompt, tap one reason, done. About two minutes, and you earn on every review.

  • He never sees who reviewed him. Reviews only display once at least two reviewers agree — no single opinion is ever identifiable. That's a hard guarantee, not a policy.
  • You can't be quoted, because you never type. Score plus reason chip — there's no free text anywhere in reviews.
  • Your Taste. The more you review, the more fora shows you what your ratings say about your own eye — what you reward, what you punish, where you split from the panel.
  • Real people on both sides. Every reviewer and every profile passes a real-person selfie check.

Trust & safety

Honest by design. Safe by architecture.

Real-person verified

Every reviewer and every submitter passes a selfie check. No bots, no catfish, both directions.

No free text, ever

Feedback is a score plus a preset reason. The language to write an insult doesn't exist in the product.

Anonymity, enforced

Reviews display only when 2+ reviewers agree — mathematically, no single voice is ever identifiable.

Every photo scanned

All images are content-scanned before they're ever stored. Nothing unsafe enters the deck.

18+ only

fora is for adults. Age-gated at signup, both sides.

FAQ

Fair questions.

Is it anonymous?+
Yes — absolutely, in both directions. He never learns who reviewed him; reviews only display once at least two reviewers agree, so no single opinion is ever identifiable. And his profile is only ever seen by his panel — never public, never browsable.
Will people be mean?+
They can't write anything. Reviews are a score and a choice from preset reasons — there's no free text anywhere. A score can be honestly negative; it can't be an insult.
Who reviews my profile?+
Five verified women, each rating independently — every photo, every prompt. What you get back is a real spread of takes, not one loud opinion.
How long does it take?+
Usually within a day — it depends on the queue. You'll see your panel fill in as reviews land.
What does it cost?+
fora runs on credits. Your welcome grant covers your first runs, so you'll see a full read before you ever pay. Reviewers never pay for anything — they earn.
